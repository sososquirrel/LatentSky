## Long thoughts (end of day)
1. **R3's story changed shape today.** The July pipeline handicapped diffusion twice
   (t_start too small; error-of-the-mean scoring), so the draft "+2 h in τ_ρ" is not
   trustworthy in either direction. But the 6 h saturation suggests the honest R3 may
   be two-sided: diffusion, properly sampled, restores small-scale structure at short
   and medium leads (ratio ≈1 achievable ≤3 h) yet hits an intrinsic ceiling at 6 h —
   it cannot invent the rainy area the deterministic warm start lost. "Where and by
   how much diffusion extends T(L)" may resolve into "fully at L,lead where the
   forecast still carries the systems; not at all once the area itself is gone."
   That is a physically meaningful, publishable answer either way.
2. **Expect per-draw detection scores to drop.** Sharp single draws will lose loose
   IoU matches that the smooth mean field kept (same mechanism as the persistence
   pitfall, inverted). The decomposition should show τ_POD possibly *shrinking* under
   the new protocol while τ_ρ and spectra improve — if so, the paper must present
   both, not cherry-pick. This is the main reason the re-derivation must go through
   sensitivity before any FRAME status moves.
3. **Sampling coherence is now the top technical debt**: independent per-tile chains
   decohere as t_start grows (patchwork at 999, plausibly capping the 6 h ratio).
   Full-mosaic single-chain sampling (256×1408, batch 1) is feasible on an A100 and
   would settle it; alternatively overlap-blend. Do this before locking t*(lead).
4. **R1 remains untouched** — persistence α is still the oldest open number, and it
   needs the metrics module (`_archive/latentsky/scale_analysis.py` → src with
   tests) before anything can be re-fit. Next session: metrics rebuild first, then
   evaluation with per-draw scoring + t*(lead), then R1/R2/R3 re-derivation in one
   pipeline. WB2/I1 untouched today, deliberately.
5. **Method note**: today produced two corrections of my own earlier claims (the
   split-mismatch retraction; the failed full-field noise rule). Both were caught by
   reading code / running the check rather than trusting a plausible story — the
   journal records both, and that is exactly the discipline FRAME is for.
