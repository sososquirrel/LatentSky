## Night: first experiment — tstart_protocol (job 5805615) — supported
- Hypothesis: t_start=100 + 3-draw mean throttles the diffusion model; sampling protocol
  was tuned on pixel R² (a double-penalty metric). `experiments/tstart_protocol/`.
- Result (lead 3 h, piece 8, single draws, λ<1000 km power ratio to truth): two-head
  0.57; t_start 50/100/250/500 → 0.61/0.70/0.89/0.96; t_start=999 diverges (ratio 98,
  wet frac 94 %, per-tile patchwork from independent tile chains). Mean-of-3 at 100:
  0.64. Wet frac at 500: −8 % vs truth; field max overshoots (22.6 vs 14.0 mm).
- Conclusion: supported — the protocol, not the trained model, was suppressing the
  generative gain. Archived spectra figure (diffusion ≈ regression at short leads) is a
  protocol artifact, to be re-examined.
- Sophie's follow-up hypothesis: t_start should grow with lead. Rule under test
  (job 5805622, `run_tstart_by_lead.py`): t*(lead) = smallest t with
  (1−ᾱ_t) ≥ ᾱ_t·MSE_z(lead). Verification at leads 0.25/1/6 h.
- Model-reference example regenerated at lead 3 h; protocol-revision note added to page.
