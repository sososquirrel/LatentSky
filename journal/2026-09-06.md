# 2026-09-06 — page interactivity: the experiment as an explorable

Objective: make §1 of `docs/model_reference.html` teach the experiment itself,
interactively (Sophie-driven, pre-draft polish).

## Lead-time explorer (`docs/make_lead_explorer.py`)
- Final design after four iterations: **one composite frame per lead** — the ten
  input channels on a cool background (rain_log1p blue-framed as the nested
  persistence input) and a warm OUTPUT block with the truth at +L and Δ vs now;
  buttons (+15 min … +6 h) and ▶ play swap the whole frame. Fixed canvas
  (mplstyle's `savefig.bbox: tight` had to be overridden — it cropped frames to
  different sizes and made the layout jump), equal box sizes by construction,
  per-panel horizontal colorbars below every box.
- Iterations rejected on Sophie's review: separate output panel column (boxes
  unequal), 4×3 input grid (holes), vertical colorbars. Lesson: for interactive
  swaps, compose everything into one figure — equality and stability come free.
- Static "One training sample" figure removed (explorer supersedes it; colormap
  key folded into the intro). Numbers surfaced: |Δ|/now volume = 0.66 at
  +15 min → 1.57 at +6 h.
