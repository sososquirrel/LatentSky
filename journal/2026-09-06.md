## Flow-matching pedagogy, final form (§4)
- Iterated with Sophie from theory-prose → four-row process figure → the
  final **six-step picture book** on the example case (`make_flow_steps.py`):
  problem / sprinkle / crossfade / the one question / walk there / two
  sprinkles-two truths (draws 13.7, 15.6 mm vs truth 10.5).
- Her questions hardened the text, each now answered on the page:
  (a) step 4 asks for *speed*, not position — constant along the crossfade,
  so the target truth − x₀ (not truth − x_t) serves every t;
  (b) why re-ask 32 times: the conditional-average field legitimately
  changes as the frame commits — curved rides from straight training lines;
  proven by `flow_step5b.png` (answers differ ~14 %/quarter-ride, changes
  live on storm edges only, mean of 32 answers = x(1) − x₀ to 0.002 rms);
  (c) t is an input, never inferred; sampler walks FORWARD with a plus sign.
