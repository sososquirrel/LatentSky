%% ===================== KEY POINTS (three, <= 140 chars each) ================
\begin{keypoints}
\item The detection horizon of km-scale precipitation grows as a power law of
system size, $T(L) \propto L^{1.8}$, from 0.5 h at 30 km to a day at 500 km
\item A rectified flow trained on the forecast's own measured error removes the
conditional-mean bias and beats the persistence null at every scale
\item Operational AI forecasts share the same size-dependent limits; sampling
restores representation, small-system placement remains unsolved
\end{keypoints}
